3. The one question open right now

The prototype is a DSH plugin and already has the plumbing. Do we start from it, or start clean and borrow from it?

A. Fork the prototype

Copy all 30k lines. Rip out GitHub/Feishu. Rename Ticket→Issue, Executor→Bot everywhere. Replace its panel with our screens.

Day 2: something runs — but it's their coding bot.
Week 6: still renaming and untangling.

RECOMMENDED

B. Start clean, lift the hard parts

Our packages, our 5 words from line 1. Copy ~5 files (worker launch, wake-up, durable queue, presets, slot registration) with credit.

Week 1: nothing to click yet.
Week 2: Captain + Issues board running, ours.
Every week after: faster.

C. Use it as-is + new UI

Doesn't work. Its work only comes from GitHub, and only a DSH coding session can do it. No Grok Bot, no routines, no chat.

Suggested first move either way: install DSH on the leader and run the prototype once, so we see its panel and worker flow with our own eyes (1 hour). Then pick A or B with evidence.
